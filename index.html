GOOD TO KNOW

MiniMuse image editor: common questions.

How do I edit a MiniMuse screenshot?

Open Upload & edit, select your image and use a lasso, magic wand or mask brush to isolate an area. Adjust its color or combine it with your own PNG layers, then download the result. Use images you are permitted to edit.

Can I remove a MiniMuse image background?

Solid or near-solid backgrounds can be removed with color tolerance controls. Complex backgrounds need manual outline and brush refinement; there is no automatic AI character cutout.

Can I use this without uploading anything?

Yes. Try Willow or choose from the available original CC0 starter pieces. The library is small, and you can add your own PNG, JPEG or WebP at any time.

Can I repair just one image?

Yes. Use Upload & edit for background cleanup, edge repair, manual selections and local color changes. Download a single PNG without finishing a character or saving a project.

Does it automatically replace clothes in a flat image?

No. Use a manual selection for local color edits, or add a separate clothing PNG and position it yourself. The editor cannot reconstruct hidden body or hair pixels.

Can MiniMuse directly open the ZIP or project backup?

These are not official game formats. Extract the asset ZIP and import individual PNGs in the app. Open .mmstudio project backups here to continue editing.

Is this the official MiniMuse game?

No. MiniMuse Tools is an independent art workspace, not operated or endorsed by Studio Sirenia. Use the official links to get the game.
