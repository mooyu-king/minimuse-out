Before you export

A finished PNG is a flat image. A .mmstudio backup preserves your layers and edits. An asset ZIP is an organizer: extract it and import the individual PNGs into MiniMuse, then check their size and position in the app. It is not an official game package.

Do I need to finish every part before downloading?

No. Download a single repaired piece, your complete image, or a ZIP of the included layers at any stage. Project backup and device autosave are optional.

Can this automatically change clothes in a finished picture?

Use a manual outline or selection brush for local edits, or position your own clothing PNG above the image. This editor does not automatically segment a character, invent hidden body pixels or fit clothing to a pose.
