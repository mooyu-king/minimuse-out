MiniMuse Studio Mode Guide

Studio Mode is easiest when you build from back to front: background, characters, props, then dialogue and finishing details.

Last reviewed September 17, 2026 · Verify steps in your current app version

Build one readable scene

Avoid filling every corner. Leave room around faces and dialogue.

  1. Choose a background.
  2. Place the main character and set the pose.
  3. Add supporting characters or props.
  4. Arrange layers and lock finished objects.
  5. Add short dialogue or text, then save or export using the options in your version.

Create, repair and combine locally

Upload your artwork to clean an edge, select a region or combine it with other pieces. Download a single PNG whenever it is ready, or keep building the complete picture.
