VERIFIED TASK GUIDE

Edit Custom Clothes for MiniMuse

A clothing PNG and a finished character picture need different workflows. Independent pieces can be moved and layered directly. Clothing embedded in a flat picture needs a manual region selection for local edits.

Last reviewed September 17, 2026 · Verify steps in your current app version

Recolor without changing the face

In Inspect & Repair, draw an outline around the clothing or paint a selection. Choose the source and replacement colors. Hue, saturation and lightness apply within the selection; protect individual colors when the drawing uses shared highlight or skin colors.

Add an independent garment

Upload a transparent garment as Top or Bottom. Use scale, position, rotation and layer order to align it. The simple taper controls are manual shape adjustments. They do not understand a pose, automatically dress a person or reconstruct missing arms.

Keep reusable variants

Save a color variant before trying another palette. Palette mappings can be reused on another piece, but preview the affected areas because similar RGB colors may represent different objects. Download a garment on its own or keep building a complete look.

Create, repair and combine locally

Upload your artwork to clean an edge, select a region or combine it with other pieces. Download a single PNG whenever it is ready, or keep building the complete picture.
