MiniMuse Beginner Guide

Start with an official store build, make one Muse, save it, and then create a small Studio scene before exploring advanced controls.

Last reviewed September 17, 2026 · Verify steps in your current app version

Your first session

Finish one small outcome before changing every option.

  1. Install from an official link and verify Studio Sirenia as the developer.
  2. Create a Muse and adjust a few visible features.
  3. Save and reopen the character.
  4. Open Studio and place the character in a simple scene.
  5. Add one prop or text element, then save again.

A useful next step

Learn layer order before importing complex custom art. It prevents most covering and positioning confusion.

Create, repair and combine locally

Upload your artwork to clean an edge, select a region or combine it with other pieces. Download a single PNG whenever it is ready, or keep building the complete picture.
