Remove a White Background from MiniMuse Artwork

A white rectangle is often part of the image itself. Asset Studio can remove a solid edge-connected background, while manual selections help protect the parts you want to keep.

Last reviewed September 17, 2026 · Verify steps in your current app version

Start with the exact file you will use

Open Upload & edit, confirm you may edit the artwork and choose its PNG, JPEG or WebP file. Inspect it over both white and dark preview backgrounds. The checkerboard is only a viewing aid; it is never written into your PNG.

  1. Select the layer and open Inspect & Repair.
  2. Choose the background color with the eyedropper, then use the picked color in the repair panel.
  3. Enable Remove edge-connected background and increase tolerance gradually.
  4. Compare Before / After and check white clothing or light hair before exporting.

When the automatic result needs help

A background connected to white parts of the drawing may remove too much. Draw an outline or use the selection brush to limit removal. Use Erase and Restore to refine the mask. Defringe is a suggestion-based color repair for semi-transparent edges, not proof that every halo is gone.

Save a useful result now

Download Current PNG even if other layers are unfinished. Turn on transparent trimming and safe padding for a compact asset. A complex scene may need careful manual work; this tool does not automatically identify a person or recreate hidden hair.

Create, repair and combine locally

Upload your artwork to clean an edge, select a region or combine it with other pieces. Download a single PNG whenever it is ready, or keep building the complete picture.
