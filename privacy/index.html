PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Image tools run in your browser. We do not receive, save or view the images you select for local processing.

Local processing

Selected files are decoded with browser APIs and remain on the device unless you independently share the exported result. File names, image content, Blob URLs and local paths are not included in analytics.

Embedded games

The Dressmaker page loads a third-party browser game from static.kingdomofmarionettes.org only after you press Play. That host receives connection information and the referring site origin, and the game may use its own browser storage. Game saves are separate from MiniMuse Tools projects. Gameplay videos use YouTube embedded players, which may receive connection information when loaded. External game availability and data handling are controlled by their providers.

Advertising

This site loads a third-party advertising script from abscloud.org after the page becomes interactive. The advertising service may receive technical request data such as IP address, browser and device information, page URL and referrer, and may use cookies or similar identifiers for advertising delivery and measurement. Advertisements may lead to external pages or windows whose content and data handling are controlled by their providers. Browser privacy controls or content blockers may prevent loading.

Traffic analytics

This site loads an analytics script from data.1back.link/js/app.js, configured for minimusetools.com, with requests sent to https://data.1back.link/api/send. The service may process page URLs, referrers and limited technical information to measure site traffic. The editor does not attach your selected images, file names, masks, palettes or saved project contents to analytics events. Avoid putting private information in page URLs.

Optional device saves

Asset Studio keeps work in memory unless you enable Keep on this device. When enabled, IndexedDB stores your project, source images, masks, layer positions, palettes and edit settings in this browser. You can separately choose Save palette across projects to store a palette on this device, and clear that palette library from the color panel. This is not a cloud upload. Clearing site data can remove those saves. Turning off saving stops future writes but does not delete existing projects; use Clear saved projects to remove them.

Settings and backups

localStorage holds preview preferences and recent-project IDs and times, not image pixels. Downloading a .mmstudio backup is your choice and creates a file through your browser. We cannot verify that you keep the downloaded file. Open Project reads only the file you select. Backup files can contain your original images; share them only when you intend to.

External services

Official store links lead to third parties with their own policies. Homepage video thumbnails load from YouTube image servers, which receive standard connection information such as your IP address. The YouTube privacy-enhanced embedded player loads only when you select Play; YouTube then processes playback data under its own privacy policy. Project images are not sent to the video player. Changes to third-party services will be reflected in this policy.
