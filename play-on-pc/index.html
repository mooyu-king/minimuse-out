How to Play MiniMuse on PC

The official Windows route is Google Play Games for PC. Availability and system requirements can change, so confirm them on the official listing.

Open the official PC listing, check compatibility, install Google Play Games, then install MiniMuse inside that service. This site does not supply an installer.
